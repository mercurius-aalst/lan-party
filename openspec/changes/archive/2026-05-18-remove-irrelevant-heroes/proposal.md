## Why

Several public pages use hero sections, but they are not serving the right purpose. The sponsors and games heroes surface internal-style summary information that is not especially useful for regular visitors, while the home hero communicates relevant content without delivering the visual impact expected from the landing page.

## What Changes

- Redesign the home page hero to be a stronger visual centerpiece with a more striking presentation
- Update the home hero to use imagery, motion, layered composition, or similar visual treatment that creates a clearer “wow effect”
- Replace the sponsors page hero with a simpler visitor-facing page intro instead of a prominent hero treatment
- Replace the games overview hero with a simpler visitor-facing page intro instead of a dashboard-style hero treatment
- Align the top sections of these pages more closely with the purpose of each page rather than repeating a loosely shared pattern

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `page-hero-experience`: Define how public-facing page heroes should prioritize relevant visitor information and page-appropriate visual presentation across landing and overview pages
